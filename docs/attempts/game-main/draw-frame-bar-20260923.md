# `draw_frame` replay bar call and coordinates

The retained `draw_frame` candidate used `rectfill` for the replay scroller bar at historical `main.c:2808`. The original instructions at offsets 4801–4895 call through `GFX_VTABLE +0xbc`. Allegro 4.4.1 `include/allegro/gfx.h` maps `+0xbc` to `rect`; `rectfill` is `+0x3c`. The historical line table also attributes the call to `draw.inl:112`, the inline `rect` definition. This is a wrong renderer call in the candidate, even though both routines accept the same argument shape.

The same original block adds `0x13` and `0x14` to stack `cx` (`-0x178(%ebp)`) for its two y coordinates. The retained candidate added `0x1d` and `0x1e`, shifting the outline by 10. The candidate now calls `rect` with `cx + 0x14` and `cx + 0x13`. `takeover-draw-bar-coordinates-20260923` separately records the coordinate correction; `takeover-draw-bar-rect-20260923` records the call correction. The latter compiled candidate calls through `+0xbc` and is 8,510/8,518 bytes. It remains `DIFFER`, with no production status change. The combined historical-order unit keeps isolated `new_game` strictly exact and `run_demo` different.

An independent vtable-call inventory found one more supported draw path missing from that retained source: the original has four calls through `+0x50` (`draw_sprite_h_flip`), while the candidate had three. Original offset 8237 is the `sx != 0` arm of the overlay draw at line 2638. The candidate's comments acknowledged this arm but omitted it because adding it increased compiled size. That size-driven omission is not valid reconstruction. The original edge==0 and edge==1 routes also enter a single shared overlay block at offset 3115; the candidate had duplicated its C source to stay close in byte count. `takeover-draw-shared-flip-20260923` restores the flip arm and the single shared block. The resulting candidate is 8,175/8,518 and remains `DIFFER`, but its virtual-call count now equals the original for every observed slot: `+0x44` 18, `+0x48` 18, `+0x50` 4, `+0xa4` 2, and `+0xbc` 1. Call-count equality is only a structural diagnostic; instruction, relocation, and whole-TU equality are still unproved.

The original has direct-call multiplicity equal to this candidate (64 calls). The main remaining annotated deficits are lines 2606 and 2638, at 95 and 75 bytes respectively, but those counts include compiler block duplication. Original offsets 1952, 3406, and 6066 write `1` to `%esi` within the DWARF `p_im` location ranges. The current candidate does not reliably account for their conditions. The absent `custom.frame[0]` null guard remains unsupported; the p_im writes are a separate unresolved fact. Trace predecessors and variable lifetimes before adding any assignment.

The predecessor trace locates three `p_im=1` writes. Original offset 1917 resets `ply->frame` on the `+0.2` speed route and writes `p_im=1` at 1952; offset 3384 does likewise on the `-0.2` route and writes at 3406. An initial interpretation coupled offset 6066 solely to the overflow reset at 6032. `takeover-draw-p-im-reset-20260923` and `takeover-draw-p-im-separate-speed-20260923` record that trial. The latter still emitted only two candidate `mov $1,%esi` instructions, versus three original instructions. A deeper trace showed that `jle` at 6030 skips the reset but lands at 6051 and still writes `p_im=1` at 6066. The trial's placement was wrong and is reverted in the retained source. Original status-zero path at 2256–2329 can also reset `frame` and enter the edge path without a `p_im=1` write. Reconstruct these branches together before restoring the assignments; the old probes are diagnostic failed experiments, not accepted recovery.

The original branch at offset 2631 tests `p_im` and jumps to the edge-sprite block at 2329 when it is zero. Calls on that edge route jump to offset 2819, after the ordinary rotate/frame sprite call. The previous candidate inverted the condition and fell through to draw an additional sprite. `takeover-draw-p-im-exclusive-edge-20260923` tested the observed exclusive paths. After reverting the unsupported reset-placement trial, `takeover-draw-exclusive-no-guess-p-im-20260923` compiles the retained candidate at 8,153/8,518 bytes, still `DIFFER`; the whole historical-order unit remains 62/82 exact with no loss. This is control-flow evidence, not a size-based promotion. The status-zero bypass and the original three separate `p_im=1` sites remain unresolved prerequisites for exact `draw_frame` matching.

The speed predicate at line 2597 was also inverted in the candidate. On the positive route, original `fldl 0.2; fucompp; sahf; jbe` at 1900–1911 skips the reset when `sx>=0.2`; on the negative route, original `fldl -0.2; fxch; fucompp; sahf; jbe` at 3365–3378 skips it when `sx<=-0.2`. Thus the reset occurs inside the open `(-0.2, 0.2)` band on those routes. `takeover-draw-frame-speed-band-20260923` corrects that predicate in the retained candidate, while leaving the unresolved status-zero routing separate. It compiles to 8,145/8,518 bytes, still `DIFFER`, with the combined 62/82 exact set unchanged. Status zero enters a separate `(-0.02, 0.02)` comparison at 2256–2308; this branch can reset `frame` and go straight to the edge-sprite block without the `p_im` test at 2631. It must be recovered before treating the current C block as complete.

`draw-frame-status-zero-trial.c` is a separate diagnostic attempt at that routing. It represents the narrow speed band with a direct jump to the edge sprite and gives the outer band a `p_im=1` route, then treats `p_im!=1` as an unconditional frame reset. `takeover-draw-status-zero-route-trial-20260923` compiles to 8,327/8,518 bytes and retains the same 62 exact functions, but the candidate emits four `mov $1,%esi` writes where the original has three. Its source shape is therefore not accepted into `draw_frame-merged.c`. The trial remains a concrete CFG experiment with an object hash in the probe record; its nearer byte size is not evidence of correctness. Next identify which predecessor should share a `p_im=1` block and trace the status-zero edge join before changing the retained body.
