## What the saved evidence actually distinguishes

The original EXE has two direct **memory** comparisons of plain `int collision_type` at `0x4120e5` and `0x412591`, then a separate `mov 0x4dd140,%eax` at the one shared jump-table dispatch `0x41259e`. The original DWARF/line map assigns all three to `main.c:3814`; its global DIE is signed `int`, with no volatile qualifier. On either valid edge, no call or store intervenes between comparison and reload. Only the branch at `0x4120ec` and fallthrough after `0x412598` enter the dispatch in the decoded PE. Thus the machine reload is an optimizer choice, not a source-semantics requirement established by a visible mutation. An inserted volatile access, dummy clobber, or duplicate dispatch has no historical support.

The retained source has **one** `switch(collision_type)` after `add_floor`, with an OR short-circuit predecessor. Its optimized tree at `083t.crited` and `084t.pre` has one selector read. By `181r.csa`, GCC has lowered it to a direct memory comparison plus a distinct selector load (`insn 927` and `5809`) feeding `tablejump_1`. Its object has two `_collision_type` relocations at function offsets `2036` and `4289`. This demonstrates that one source switch can produce a check/reload pair without explicit source guards. ## Inference and next gate

The original pattern is **consistent with late duplication of a switch-generated bounds-check block** after tree PRE: one shared check/reload pair exists in the retained source's CSA dump, whereas the PE has two predecessor-specific checks and one shared reload. This is an inference, not proof of a historical pass or original C spelling. It is more tightly supported than explicit source guards: those cause PRE to reuse the checked values and lose the third load. A different source/TU CFG around the predecessor join could cause the late duplication, but no currently evidenced declaration, alias, store, or CFG edge identifies such a specific change. The existing OR condition already encodes the decoded short-circuit edges, and the three explicit-guard forms exhaust the source-backed guard hypothesis. No further C overlay was run.
